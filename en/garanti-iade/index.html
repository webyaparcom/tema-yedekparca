Warranty and returns

Parts are clearly labelled “OEM” (approved by the vehicle manufacturer) or “equivalent”; the word “original” is used only for OEM parts.

Parts can be returned within 14 days provided they are unused and in unopened packaging. For electrical parts, returns after installation are accepted only with a fault report.

The warranty period varies by part group and is stated on the invoice.
